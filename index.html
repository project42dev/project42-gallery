Architecture & Contract Rules

What You Can and Cannot Customize

To ensure platform portability, security, and curriculum integrity across organizations, the Theme Engine enforces strict customization boundaries:

Customizable Theme Surface

  • Colors & Tokens: Full palette customization via tokens.css (primary, surfaces, text).
  • Typography: Custom font families (Google Fonts or local web fonts).
  • Brand Assets: Custom mark.svg vector logo, favicon, and hero.png artwork.
  • Achievement Badges: Custom SVG milestone vectors for 4 curriculum progression tiers.
  • Navigation & Links: Institution support links and custom footer destinations.

Immutable Platform Core

  • NIST / ISO Citations: Fact-checking citations and evidence data remain immutable.
  • Curriculum Schemas: Core learning schemas (module.json, catalog.json) cannot be overridden.
  • Transcript Signatures: Cryptographic verification hashes cannot be modified.
  • Security Assertions: Prompt injection defense assertions remain strictly enforced.
